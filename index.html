Less timeline. More content.

MT-CLIP is built around one simple idea: you shouldn't have to watch an entire video twice just to find five moments worth posting.

AI CLIPPING

Find the good parts.

AI analyzes the video's context and looks for moments with hooks, payoff, emotion, humor, insight, or strong standalone value.

SHORT-FORM

One video, multiple formats.

Create landscape clips and vertical 9:16 versions designed for Shorts, TikTok, and Reels.

LOCAL AI

Your machine. Your model.

Run AI locally with Ollama. No subscription is required just to analyze your videos.

SUBTITLES

Make every word pop.

Generate animated subtitles and customize their appearance so your clips feel ready for short-form platforms.

VIRALENHANCE

Add trend context.

Optionally enrich AI analysis with trend signals from YouTube, Google Trends, Reddit, and TikTok.

FREE

No AI API bill.

Use local AI with Ollama and keep the core workflow free. API-powered trend features are optional.
